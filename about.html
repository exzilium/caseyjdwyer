My Career Journey

Behind the Mic

With over a decade of experience, my career started with a love for theatrical performance and evolved into a passion for the nuances of audio storytelling. My dedicated, broadcast-quality home studio allows me to deliver pristine audio for any project on tight deadlines.

I specialize in capturing the exact tone required—whether that's the relatable neighbor for a commercial spot, or the commanding gravitas of a video game villain.

Process & Professionalism

Voice acting is as much about listening as it is about speaking. I pride myself on taking direction well during live directed sessions and thoroughly dissecting scripts to find the core intention of the copy.

I've had the privilege of voicing campaigns for Random Encounters, Hulu, and more, bringing dozens of characters to life.
